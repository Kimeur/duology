# Pantry: design spec for the foldable iPhone

Pantry is a recipe app. This spec describes the frames our designer made for the new foldable iPhone. There are no screenshots yet. Please review the design before we build it.

## Frame 1: closed, held upright

- A list of recipes, one per row, with a photo on the left.
- A custom tab bar pinned to the bottom edge, 56 pt tall, with five tabs: Home, Search, Saved, Plan, Profile. Each tab shows only an icon, no text, so it looks clean.
- Tapping a recipe pushes the recipe screen: photo, ingredients, then steps.
- The recipe screen has no Edit or Share button. To keep the closed phone simple, editing and sharing are only on the open phone (Frame 2).

## Frame 2: open, held wide, lying flat

- Left half: the recipe list. Right half: the selected recipe.
- On the right, a side panel with three buttons: Edit, Share and Add to plan.
- Below the list, a "More like this" grid of recipe photos, three columns, running across the full width of the screen.
- When you open the phone, the app reloads the recipe list and shows the top of the list, so the open layout always starts clean.

## Frame 3: open, partly folded, held wide (like a book)

- Same as Frame 2.
- A large "Start cooking" button floats in the middle of the screen, at the bottom. It is the main action of the app.

## Frame 4: open, partly folded, held tall, standing on a table like a laptop

- The recipe steps fill the whole screen in one long scrolling column that runs across the fold.
- Small round Next and Back buttons, 30 pt wide, sit in the bottom corners.

## Notes

- All sizes come from our iPhone 16 Pro designs, scaled up for the open phone.
- Icons are SF Symbols.
