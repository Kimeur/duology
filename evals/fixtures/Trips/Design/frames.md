# Trips: home screen

Two frames from the design file, exported as notes. Sizes are in points.

## Frame A: closed phone, upright

- Side margins: 16 pt.
- Title "Upcoming trips": bold, 28 pt.
- One column of trip cards, 16 pt apart.
- Card: a 140 pt photo, then the city (17 pt semibold, black), the dates (13 pt, #B3B3B3) and a note of up to two lines (13 pt, #737373). 14 pt padding. White card.
- Card corners: the same large rounded corners as the phone's screen, so the cards echo the hardware.
- Heart button in the top-right corner of the photo: a 30 pt circle.
- "Plan a trip" button after the last card, full width.

## Frame B: open phone, held wide

- Side margins: 24 pt.
- Title: bold, 34 pt.
- Three columns of cards, 16 pt apart.
- Card: a 160 pt photo, city 20 pt, dates and note 15 pt, 20 pt padding.
- Everything else as in Frame A.

## Notes

- The open frame is the closed frame scaled up, with more columns.
- Background: the system grouped background.
