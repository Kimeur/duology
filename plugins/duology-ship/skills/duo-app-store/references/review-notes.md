# App Review notes for iPhone Duo

The notes field in App Review Information is read by a person with little time. Say where the Duo experience is, how to reach it, and anything they need to get there. Facts only: no marketing.

## Template

```text
iPhone Duo: <one sentence on what changes when the phone opens>.

To see it:
1. Open <screen> on the closed phone (outer display).
2. Open the phone: <what happens, e.g. the list and the detail sit side by side>.
3. <Book or tabletop, if the app does something there: e.g. half fold and stand the phone up; the video stays above the fold and the controls move below.>

Poses supported: closed, open (held wide and tall)<, book><, tabletop>. State is kept when the phone folds or unfolds.
<Sign-in: demo account and password, or "no account needed".>
<Anything else needed: sample data switch, region, hardware.>
```

## Good habits

- Name the screen exactly as the app labels it.
- One numbered path per feature, three or four steps at most.
- If a feature needs the camera, a second window or Split View, say so and how to start it.
- Keep it current: update the notes when a release changes what the open phone does.
